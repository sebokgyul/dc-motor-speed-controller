# Responsible AI-Use Record

AI assistance was used as a development tool for this portfolio extension. Generated suggestions were treated as untrusted until reviewed against the existing architecture and checked by compilation, tests, deterministic output comparison, or a clearly identified manual step. AI was not used at runtime by the motor simulation or dashboard.

Status meanings in this record are: **accepted** means an automated engineering gate currently supports the change; **modified** means the initial suggestion was changed to fit the repository or safety boundary; **rejected** means it was deliberately not used; **pending** means owner or manual verification has not happened. None of these labels substitutes for repository-owner review.
